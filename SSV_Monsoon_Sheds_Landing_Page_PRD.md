## 1. Executive Summary

Shah Shivji Valji & Company, established in 1910 and carrying **116+ years of industry experience**, is India's leading temporary storage solution provider and one of the country's largest exporters and suppliers of waterproof tarpaulins. This document defines the full product requirements for a high-conversion landing page dedicated to the company's flagship product — **Monsoon Sheds on Hire**.

The landing page is the primary digital entry point for industrial buyers, procurement managers, and project heads seeking temporary weather-protection shed solutions across India. It must communicate trust, capability, and urgency — driving prospects to either request a free quote or initiate a WhatsApp conversation.

### Primary Objectives

- Generate qualified leads through a quote request form and WhatsApp CTA
- Communicate Shah Shivji Valji's unique value proposition: 116 years of expertise, pan-India reach, and in-house manufacturing capability
- Showcase key applications and trusted client logos to build credibility
- Guide the visitor through a structured journey from awareness to conversion
- Achieve a target conversion rate of **3–5%** from organic and paid traffic

### Product: Monsoon Sheds on Hire

Temporary monsoon sheds are cost-effective, weatherproof enclosures erected on a contract/hire basis to protect business assets — raw materials, heavy machinery, finished products — from monsoon rains, dust, wind, and direct sunlight. The sheds are fully customisable in size, material, and configuration. Being hire-based, they eliminate capital expenditure for clients while providing the same protection as a permanent structure.

### Types of Monsoon Sheds Offered

| Shed Type | Structure | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary Storage Sheds | Bamboo & wooden pole | Immediate, low-cost storage needs for any industry |
| MS Widespan Sheds | Steel frame — column-free | Bulk raw materials, heavy & mobile machinery |
| Hybrid Sheds | Steel frame + traditional materials | Long-term temporary storage of bulk goods |
